# Brag Plan: J.A.R.V.I.S. (Chaotic Edition — 35 Seconds)

## What is this app?
J.A.R.V.I.S. is a 100% offline, privacy-first personal desktop AI assistant inspired by Iron Man and built on Stanford's OpenJarvis architecture, featuring biometric speaker voice verification, local Ollama intelligence, universal Windows automation, and autonomous on-device QLoRA fine-tuning.

## The angle
Most AI assistants are fragile cloud wrappers that stop working without internet and leak private voice recordings to cloud servers. J.A.R.V.I.S. proves you can run a true Iron Man-grade assistant entirely on your local PC — with speaker biometric security, sub-20ms deterministic reflex fallback, and closed-loop self-learning on consumer GPUs.
